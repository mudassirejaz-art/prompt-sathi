# Real-world work

## Client delivery update

**User** — “Client ko update deni hai. Delivery do din late hai. English mein short message chahiye.”

The goal and output are clear. Propose the draft directly. “Main ek short English draft likhun jo do din ki delay bataye aur reason ya exact date guess na kare?”

After approval, deliver the draft:

> Hi [Client Name], I wanted to let you know that your delivery will be two days later than planned. I’m sorry for the delay. Thank you for your patience.

This draft is ready to copy after the client name is filled in. If the user asks to send it, confirm the recipient and final text before sending.

## YouTube request with two possible goals

**User** — “Mera YouTube SEO theek karo.”

Ask “Ek video ka title aur thumbnail improve karna hai, ya poore channel ka review chahiye?” If they choose one video, ask for its topic or link and any competitor links they have. Once those arrive, name the specific public sources and ask approval for the review.

After approval, give a small set of usable outputs. Pair each title with a thumbnail concept and explain why the pair fits the audience. Base the description on what the user says the video actually covers. If the content is unknown, label suggestions as provisional and avoid promising features, results, or chapters the user has not confirmed.
